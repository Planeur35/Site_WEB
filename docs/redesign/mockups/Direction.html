Ciel & marine

White and sky blue carry the mood. One deeper marine blue marks the main action, and a soft sand dot is the only warm note. Large photos, real type, almost no boxes.

Quand il n’y a pas de photo

Instead of clip-art clouds: a pale sky and a single thin flight trace — a thermal climb, then a long glide, ending on a small sand-coloured dot. The trace is picked from the item’s title, so a list of photo-less news never repeats. Gliders without a photo show their contest letters.

Mouvement

One easing, “planeur”: a quick start, then a long glide — cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1). CSS only, plus one small IntersectionObserver. All of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

Fondu cielHero photos cross-fade over 1.2 s every 7 s, with a slow 1.04 → 1 drift. Visible pause button. Reduced motion: first photo only, arrows to change.
MontéeBlocks rise 12 px and fade in over 600 ms when they enter the screen, once, 60 ms apart in a row.
CompteurKey figures count up over 900 ms the first time they are seen. The final number is in the HTML for screen readers and no-JS.
Réponse160 ms on hover and press: buttons darken, arrows slide 4 px, photos scale 1.03 inside their frame.
